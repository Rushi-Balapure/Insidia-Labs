# 0005 Licenses and attribution

Insidia is Apache-2.0. Direct dependencies are MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, or ISC. MPL-2.0 and BSD infrastructure is allowed after review and attribution. GPL, AGPL, SSPL, Elastic, and BUSL fail CI. The runner ships no scanner code. Attribution is a release requirement: `NOTICE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` list every bundled dependency, and reports name the engine behind each finding.
